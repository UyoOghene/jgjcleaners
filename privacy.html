Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website and services, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable Ontario law.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the website located at jgjanitorial.netlify.app (the "Site") and to personal information we collect when you request a quote, book a cleaning service, apply for a job, or otherwise interact with us in person, by phone, by email, by text/WhatsApp, or through the Site. It does not apply to third-party websites we link to.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Quote & booking details: name, phone number, email address, service address or general location, preferred date, service type, and any details you choose to include in a message.
  • Job applications: name, email, phone (optional), and the experience description you provide.
  • Communications: the content of emails, WhatsApp messages, calls, and texts you send us.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, referring URL, and timestamps. Our hosting and form providers may log this data for security and delivery purposes.
  • Analytics (if enabled): aggregated, non-identifying usage statistics (pages viewed, approximate region). We do not currently run advertising trackers.

2.3 Information we do not collect

We do not collect payment card numbers, government identifiers, health information, or precise GPS location through the Site. Payments, if any, are arranged offline by invoice or another channel you agree to.

3. How we use information

  • Respond to quote requests and answer questions.
  • Schedule, deliver and follow up on cleaning services.
  • Process job applications and communicate with candidates.
  • Send service-related notices (e.g., confirming a booking, rescheduling).
  • Maintain records for accounting, tax and legal compliance.
  • Protect the Site and our business against fraud, abuse and security threats.
  • Improve our services and website (using aggregated data).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Legal basis & consent

Under PIPEDA we rely on your knowledge and consent to collect, use or disclose personal information. Consent may be express (for example, submitting a form with a required checkbox or by sending us a request) or implied where reasonable (for example, replying to an email you initiated). You may withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice, by contacting us using the details in section 14. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide a service.

5. Cookies & tracking

The Site itself does not set first-party advertising cookies. Third-party services embedded on the Site — including Google Fonts (font delivery) and Formspree (form processing) — may set cookies or receive your IP address and user agent in order to function. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; blocking cookies from these providers may affect font rendering or form submission. We do not currently use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or other behavioural advertising tools. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent.

6. Sharing & service providers

We share personal information only as needed with:

  • Formspree (Formspree, Inc., United States) — to receive and route the quote and career forms on the Site.
  • Netlify (Netlify, Inc., United States) — website hosting and delivery.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC, United States) — font delivery.
  • Email, phone and messaging providers we use to communicate with you.
  • Professional advisors (accountant, lawyer) where necessary for tax, legal or insurance purposes.
  • Authorities where required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, or property.

These providers are contractually or by their own terms obligated to protect information and to use it only for the purpose it was disclosed. We do not sell or rent personal information.

7. International transfers

Our Site and some service providers are located in the United States. Information you submit through the Site may therefore be stored or processed outside Canada. Where information is transferred internationally, we take reasonable steps (including choosing reputable providers with appropriate safeguards) to ensure it receives a comparable level of protection. By using the Site and submitting a form, you acknowledge this transfer. You may instead contact us by phone or email if you prefer not to use the online form.

8. How long we keep information

  • Quote requests: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a client.
  • Job applications: up to 12 months, unless you ask us to keep them longer.
  • Client service records: up to 7 years, to meet accounting and tax obligations.
  • Site logs: retained by our hosting/form providers for a short period for security and delivery; we do not maintain our own copy.

When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including HTTPS on the Site, restricted access to inboxes and accounts, and reputable service providers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; if you have reason to believe your interaction with us is no longer secure, please contact us immediately using the details in section 14.

If a breach of security safeguards involving personal information occurs and it is reasonable in the circumstances to believe it creates a real risk of significant harm, we will report it to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and notify affected individuals as required by PIPEDA.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Withdraw consent (see section 4).
  • Ask questions or raise concerns about how we handle your information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 14. We will respond within 30 days, or explain why we need more time. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. In limited cases we may refuse access where the law requires (for example, where it would reveal another person's information or legal privilege); we will explain any refusal.

11. Children

The Site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the Site. If you believe a child has submitted information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Third-party links

The Site may link to third-party sites (for example, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Google Maps). Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. We encourage you to review their policies before providing information.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or the law. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be announced on this page. Your continued use of the Site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
